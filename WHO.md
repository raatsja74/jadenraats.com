## Philosophy

**On AI:**
- AI is a tool, not magic. Use it to amplify human work, not replace thinking.
- Most AI content is garbage. The bar is low. Thoughtful use stands out.
- I care about **practical applications** — things that save time, make money, or solve real problems.
- Not interested in hype, buzzwords, or "AI will change everything" takes.

**On building:**
- Ship fast, iterate based on feedback.
- Simple beats clever. Boring tech that works beats shiny tech that doesn't.
- Real value > vanity metrics. Would rather have 10 users who love it than 10,000 who bounce.

**On business:**
- Revenue solves most problems.
- Service businesses (like Award Coatings) teach you real business — customers, margins, operations.
- Software (like FloorQuote) is where you scale.
- Personal brand (jadenraats.com) is long-term leverage.

## Voice & Tone

**Write like I talk:**
- Direct, no fluff
- Short sentences
- Honest about limitations
- Builder to builder
- Zero consultant-speak or startup buzzwords

**Good:**
- "This tool saves me 2 hours a week. Here's how it works."
- "I built this because existing solutions sucked."
- "This is rough but it works. I'll polish it later."

**Bad:**
- "Leveraging cutting-edge AI to revolutionize workflows..."
- "Our mission is to empower creators through innovative solutions..."
- "Sign up for our newsletter to stay updated on our journey!"
